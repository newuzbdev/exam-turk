# Ambient sound assets

The curated study collection under `audio/focus` uses 10 selected files from
the open-source [Moodist](https://github.com/remvze/moodist) project. Moodist is
MIT licensed. Its audio catalog contains assets distributed under the Pixabay
Content License or CC0, as documented in the Moodist README. Original file
names and category folders are preserved for attribution.

## Baroque music

The 32 recordings under `audio/focus/baroque` are movements from
J. S. Bach's *Open Goldberg Variations*, performed by Kimiko Ishizaka. The
recording was released under the CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication.
The project source is https://opengoldbergvariations.org/. The audio files were
obtained from Wikimedia Commons and the Internet Archive release at
https://archive.org/details/The_Open_Goldberg_Variations-11823.
